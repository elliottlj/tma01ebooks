## About these files

The text files were made from the Standard Ebooks source files (XHTML) on GitHub.
Only the main text of each novel is kept: the part, book and chapter headings and
the paragraphs. The following were removed:

- the title page, imprint, colophon and copyright statement
- endnotes and footnote markers
- front matter that comes before the story, such as epigraphs and prefaces
- formatting, which was converted to UTF-8 plain text

Any mistakes introduced by this conversion are mine, not Standard Ebooks'.
